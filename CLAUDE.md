
## Strict Formatting Rule

Conclude every response with a TL;DR section summarizing the key points in 1 sentence. If legitimately multiple threads, max of 3 sentences. STRONGLY PREFER ONE.

If my statement can be summarized in 1 sentence, I will just write it in one sentence. That alone does not require a TL;DR, since it is not long.

## American English only

Write to me in American English, never British English: defense, offense, color, favorite, analyze, center,
program. This holds for every message, report, table, chart and page shared with me. Code names and file names
already in the repository keep their spelling.

## Orientation

- Read `README.md` first (what OpenRAPM is, the five steps, the commands), then `notes/HANDOFF.md` (where the work
  stands, what is waiting on the owner, and the commands a session runs). `notes/DECISIONS.md` is the record of
  every experiment; read its measurement traps before trusting a number.
- The writing rules are in `CONTRIBUTING.md`, "The writing rules". They bind code, comments, docs and every message
  to the owner.
- Never thrash the owner's machine. Several processes at once are fine (`--jobs`, `OPENRAPM_JOBS`) while they fit
  its memory and cores; when a chain is stopped, stop its python children too.
- Never commit, push or publish (the site is `docs/`, served from `main`) without the owner's word.
