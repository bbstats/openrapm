# How the ratings may be used

The code in this repository is under the MIT license, in the file LICENSE. Fork it, change it, run it, build your own
ratings with it.

The ratings are not under the MIT license. The ratings are the numbers published at https://openrapm.com and the files
under docs/data/ in this repository. They are the work of Nathan Walker, and these terms go with them:

1. Use them and quote them, in an article, a post, a chart or your own analysis, with credit to
   "OpenRAPM by Nathan Walker" and a link to https://openrapm.com.
2. Do not republish the full table or most of it, and do not mirror the site, without permission.
3. Do not collect the site or these files in bulk by script. No crawling for AI: not for training, not for AI answers.
4. A fork or a clone of this repository carries the files under docs/data/ with it. That is fine; these terms stay
   with those files.

Ratings you compute yourself by running the code are yours.

To ask permission: twitter.com/bbstats, or an issue at github.com/bbstats/openrapm.
