vs 2026 observed. OpenRAPM's 2026 rating is used as the prediction for every team-game of the season, and a ridge is fitted to what is left over, with a free amplitude per side and a free level per team. The coefficient it returns is the part of the miss that belongs to the player, so this column is OpenRAPM against what actually happened on the court — no outside metric is involved.

vs consensus. The consensus is a GLS-weighted mean of DRIP, LEBRON, EPM, DARKO DPM, Time-Decay RAPM, and Time-Decay-Luck-Adjusted RAPM. The weighting de-duplicates the vote, so metrics that say nearly the same thing share one vote rather than each getting a full one.

The two yardsticks agree with each other at R² = 0.57 across the 339 players both cover, so they are close but not interchangeable — which is why a type can be red in one column and blue in the other.

Player types are fitted, not hand-made: a Bayesian Gaussian mixture over per-36 box rates. The line under each name is the four rates that type is furthest from the league on.
