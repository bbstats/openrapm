On-court rtg
His on-court rating: points per 100 possessions his team scored minus allowed while he was on the court. Only those possessions: nothing from when he sits. The starting point.
Teammates
Minus the combined RAPM of the four teammates on the court with him, weighted by time together. Strong teammates make it negative: they account for part of the margin.
Opponents
The combined RAPM of the five opponents on the court with him, weighted by time. Strong opponents make it positive.
Context
Minus what home court, playoffs, garbage time, the score margin and the season predict for his minutes.
Ridge penalty
The regression's pull toward zero, which keeps small samples from producing extreme ratings. Negative for good players, and larger the fewer possessions he has.

Every possession's result, measured against what its situation predicts, counts toward his RAPM with a weight the regression sets. Each piece adds up one group of possessions.

His minutes
The possessions he played. Not the same number as On-court rtg in the other split: each possession here is weighted by how much it tells the regression about him.
Team without him
His team's possessions without him, in games he played. A team that falls apart without him adds to his rating; one that stays good takes away.
Games he missed
His team's possessions in games he didn't play, in seasons he played for that team. A season missed entirely can't be placed, because injured players aren't in the box scores, so it counts under everything else.
Everything else
Every other possession in the span. It reaches his rating through his teammates' and opponents' ratings.
RAPM
Plain regularized adjusted plus-minus on points: regular season and playoffs, fit on the chosen span alone, ridge penalty 3,000 per side. Unlike the main ratings, it has no box-score prior.
Why both splits add up: by player, the five pieces are the regression's own equation for each player, and each averages zero across the league, weighted by possessions. By possession, RAPM is a weighted sum of every possession's result, so grouping the possessions splits it exactly. Every row adds up to his RAPM to rounding.
