OpenRAPM

The playoff delta is how far a postseason moved a player off his regular-season rating, not a playoff rating of its own. It is the residual of a fit that sees only playoff possessions and takes the regular-season number as its prior, so a full run is worth about a fifth of a point and the largest in thirty seasons is about one. Chosen and checked on separate halves of the seasons; it predicts the rest of a series better and puts the credit on better players.

#Player Season Offense Defense Total Poss.
A multi-stage prior-informed RAPM: a role-and-age prior, then a gradient-boosted box-score prior, then a ridge on the on-court result, chosen by out-of-season prediction of held-out games. A season's rating is fit on that season and the two before it, the earlier ones weighted a half and a quarter, with nothing after it in the fit. Source, method and every measurement: github.com/bbstats/openrapm. Architecture changes are welcome; the test that decides is scripts/45_holdout.py.
