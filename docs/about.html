Prior inputs

The box-score prior reads one season of stats, playoffs included, and nothing from any other season. "Per 100" means per 100 on-court possessions: team possessions on the offense list, opponent possessions on the defense list. Hustle stats, 2017 on, are per 100 and shown as % above or below the league average.

Padding

Over a few hundred possessions most of a rate is luck, so counting stats are padded: blended with the rate of players who played about as many possessions that season. How much is blended in is measured for each stat and each season: the more of the spread between players is luck, the more is blended in.

A bench player with 200 possessions and 2.1 steals per 100. Steals that season carry a pad of 450 possessions, and players with about 200 possessions average 1.4. Padded: 200 × 2.1 + 450 × 1.4, divided by 200 + 450, is 1.6. A starter with 3,000 possessions and the same 2.1 keeps 2.0.

Percentages are built from the padded makes and misses, then get a few league-typical attempts added on top, so a player who went 2 for 2 on threes does not read as a 100% shooter.
