Prior inputs

The box-score prior reads one season of a player's stats and nothing from his other seasons. "Per 100" means per 100 possessions he was on the court for: his team's possessions on the offense list, the opponent's possessions on the defense list.

Padding

Over a few hundred possessions most of a rate is luck, so counting stats are padded: blended with the rate of players who played about as many possessions that season. How much is blended in is measured for each stat and each season, from how well players' odd-numbered games agree with their even-numbered games.

A bench player with 200 possessions and 2.1 steals per 100. Steals that season carry a pad of 450 possessions, and players with about 200 possessions average 1.4. Padded: 200 × 2.1 + 450 × 1.4, divided by 200 + 450, is 1.6. A starter with 3,000 possessions and the same 2.1 keeps 2.0.

Percentages are built from the padded makes and misses, then get a few league-typical attempts added on top, so a player who went 2 for 2 on threes does not read as a 100% shooter.
